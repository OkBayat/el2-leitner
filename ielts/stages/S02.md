#### L0081 · Shopping for ingredients — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0080. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** ingredient, vegetable, carrot, onion, pepper, cheese.
**Collocations:** a kilo of rice; a bottle of oil.
**Grammar / discourse:** Count and mass nouns with some/any; How much/how many; containers and quantities.
**Worked diagnostic:** `How many rice do we need?` → `How much rice do we need?` Rice is a mass noun in this context; ask how much, or count bags or bowls.
**Reading:** Follow instructions, locate quantities and distinguish routines from current actions. Family: `short-answer`.
**Listening:** Complete a simple form from a two-speaker exchange and track a correction. Family: `form-completion`. **Sound focus:** Plural endings, contracted forms, weak function words and question intonation.
**Speaking:** Role-play a shop order. Ask for two ingredients, specify a quantity, then politely correct one part of your order. Answer a follow-up about payment.
**Writing:** Write 40–65 words explaining what you need for a simple meal and what you already have. Use some/any and one countable quantity.
