#### L0321 · Food waste and responsible consumption — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0320. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** surplus, shortage, expiry, storage, preservation, distribution.
**Collocations:** food waste; expiry date.
**Grammar / discourse:** Second conditional; Would + base form; hypothetical versus realistic conditions.
**Worked diagnostic:** `If shops would donate more food, less would be wasted.` → `If shops donated more food, less would be wasted.` In the standard hypothetical pattern, the if-clause uses past simple rather than would.
**Reading:** Identify paragraph purpose, paraphrase and supporting detail without searching for exact word repetition. Family: `sentence-completion`.
**Listening:** Follow everyday and study-related exchanges across a complete question block. Family: `note-completion`. **Sound focus:** Multiword chunks, reduced forms, emphasis and intelligible multi-clause delivery.
**Speaking:** Describe a local scheme that tries to solve a practical problem. Explain its goal, one difficulty and a better way to measure success. Speak for up to two minutes.
**Writing:** Write 150–200 words answering: Is collecting surplus food enough to solve food waste? Use the fictional trial as an example, distinguish prevention from redistribution and acknowledge a limitation.
