#### L0561 · Global food systems — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0560. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** interdependence, resilience, volatility, distribution, scarcity, surplus.
**Collocations:** food security; supply-chain resilience.
**Grammar / discourse:** Precision in complex noun phrases; Resolve stacked modifiers before writing; use of-phrases where clearer.
**Worked diagnostic:** `The regional food supply resilience improvement improved.` → `The resilience of the regional food supply improved.` Reduce nominal piling and make the central claim explicit.
**Reading:** Distinguish claims from evidence and interpret implication without importing outside knowledge. Family: `true-false-not-given`.
**Listening:** Follow extended argument and retain the final position after concessions and corrections. Family: `table-completion`. **Sound focus:** Flexible emphasis and phrasing; recognise unfamiliar voices without assuming a single preferred accent.
**Speaking:** Is an efficient system necessarily a reliable one? Develop a position, acknowledge a counterexample and explain what information would change your view.
**Writing:** Task 2-style practice: Some organisations aim to minimise costs, while others keep spare capacity to deal with unexpected problems. Discuss both approaches and give your opinion. Write at least 250 words.
