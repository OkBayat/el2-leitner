#### L0481 · Food policy and competing priorities — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0480. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** subsidy, regulation, taxation, incentive, affordability, availability.
**Collocations:** food policy; economic incentive.
**Grammar / discourse:** Modals and counterarguments in policy; Should/ought to; proportionate obligation and exceptions.
**Worked diagnostic:** `Labels should must be clear.` → `Labels should be clear, although the policy may need exceptions.` Do not stack these modal verbs; select one appropriate expression of obligation.
**Reading:** Track scope, qualification, author stance and similarities across paraphrases. Family: `true-false-not-given`.
**Listening:** Track agreement, disagreement and attributed opinions in educational discussions. Family: `table-completion`. **Sound focus:** Prosodic grouping, contrast, unobtrusive self-repair and intelligibility during extended explanations.
**Speaking:** Discuss whether lower prices are enough to make a service accessible. Explain two other barriers and respond to a follow-up that challenges your proposed solution.
**Writing:** Task 2-style practice: Some people believe governments should reduce the price of healthier food. Others think consumers should make food choices without government intervention. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Write at least 250 words. Do not invent research or statistics.
