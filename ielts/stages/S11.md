**Collocations:** system resilience; productive efficiency.
**Grammar / discourse:** Synthesis across evidence and constraints; Concession integrated into a single coherent position.
**Worked diagnostic:** `Greater redundancy reduces efficiency, so resilience cannot improve.` → `Greater redundancy may reduce efficiency while improving resilience.` The two outcomes can move in different directions; retain the trade-off.
**Reading:** Resolve fine distinctions, evaluate competing interpretations and retain exact scope under time pressure. Family: `matching-headings`.

**Writing:** Task 2-style practice: Organisations should always choose the most efficient way of operating, even if this leaves little spare capacity. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Write at least 250 words.
