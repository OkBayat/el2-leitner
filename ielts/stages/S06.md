#### L0401 · Agriculture and food supply — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0400. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** agriculture, cultivation, harvest, crop, yield, irrigation.
**Collocations:** crop yield; food supply.
**Grammar / discourse:** Third conditional; Past counterfactuals; had + participle and would have + participle.
**Worked diagnostic:** `If storage would have improved, less food would have spoiled.` → `If storage had improved, less food would have spoiled.` The standard counterfactual if-clause uses had improved.
**Reading:** Summarise relationships between claims, evidence and examples in extended prose. Family: `sentence-completion`.
**Listening:** Follow educational discussion and a short lecture with reduced replay support. Family: `note-completion`. **Sound focus:** Natural pacing, clause boundaries, correction without repeated restarting and accent variation.
**Speaking:** Explain why two systems can each be better on different measures. Use the irrigation comparison, then give another example. Respond to the follow-up: How should a decision-maker choose between the measures?
**Writing:** Academic Task 1-style practice: summarise the fictional data in at least 150 words. A: water 1,000, yield 200; B: water 800, yield 190. Include an overview and accurate comparisons. Do not infer costs, profitability or causation.
