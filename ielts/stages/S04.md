#### L0241 · Food choices and simple nutrition — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0240. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** nutrition, nutrient, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fibre.
**Collocations:** food label; portion size.
**Grammar / discourse:** Countability in food and abstract nouns; Few/a few; little/a little without equating their meanings.
**Worked diagnostic:** `There are little informations on the label.` → `There is little information on the label.` Information is normally uncountable; use is and do not add plural -s.
**Reading:** Track reference and cause; distinguish explicit contradiction from missing information. Family: `sentence-completion`.
**Listening:** Identify key details, speaker intentions and changes of plan in short sustained audio. Family: `note-completion`. **Sound focus:** Linking, contrastive stress, prominent content words and self-monitoring of intelligibility.
**Speaking:** Explain why two labels or prices can be difficult to compare. Give an example and identify one thing that the information does not establish. Speak for 60–90 seconds.
**Writing:** Write 100–150 words explaining the label-comparison activity. Include the initial error, the correction and one limitation. Do not give medical advice or invent nutrient figures.
