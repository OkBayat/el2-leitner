# Stage 01: Build usable sentences

Entry around 3; work towards 3.5-level demands. This is a design orientation, not a calibrated band prediction.

#### L0001 · Food and everyday meals — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** entry diagnostic / foundation bridge. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** bread, rice, water, milk, eat, drink.
**Collocations:** have breakfast; drink water.
**Grammar / discourse:** Subject + present-tense verb + object with I/you/we/they; Object nouns; receptive recognition of eats/drinks with named subjects; capital I and final full stop.
**Worked diagnostic:** `I rice eat.` → `I eat rice.` Place the finite verb after the subject in this simple statement. Rice is the object.
**Reading:** Find explicitly stated people, actions, objects and locations in short texts. Family: `short-answer`.
**Listening:** Recognise familiar words and retrieve a stated item, number or place without relying on the transcript. Family: `form-completion`. **Sound focus:** Word boundaries, stressed syllables, clear final consonants and meaningful pauses.
**Speaking:** Say what you eat and drink in the morning. Use two or three complete statements. You may invent a meal. Do not read the model.
**Writing:** Write 20–40 words about one meal. Say who eats, what they eat and what they drink. Use the six target words where relevant; do not force all of them.
