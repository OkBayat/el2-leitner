#### L0641 · Food systems under uncertainty — Form and meaning
**Purpose:** Establish a usable lexical representation and use it in a simple communicative exchange. **Prerequisite:** L0640. Introduce this subset; reuse shared vocabulary identities.
**Vocabulary:** resilience, vulnerability, diversification, contingency, buffer, volatility.
**Collocations:** supply disruption; contingency planning.
**Grammar / discourse:** Counterfactual reasoning and conditional limits; Were it not for; equivalent plain alternatives remain acceptable.
**Worked diagnostic:** `Without the reserve, the shortage will have been worse yesterday.` → `Without the reserve, the shortage would have been worse.` Use a coherent past hypothetical consequence rather than a future-perfect form.
**Reading:** Resolve ambiguity, trace alternative explanations and avoid overgeneralising a qualified statement. Family: `true-false-not-given`.
**Listening:** Infer attitude from wording and prosody while preserving what is explicitly versus implicitly supported. Family: `table-completion`. **Sound focus:** Meaningful intonation, listener-friendly pace and adaptation to unfamiliar speakers.
**Speaking:** Should decision-makers wait for certainty before acting? Give a balanced answer, distinguish useful estimates from guarantees and explain how a provisional decision can be reviewed.
**Writing:** Task 2-style practice: Some people think decisions should only be made when enough information is available. Others believe waiting for more information often causes greater problems. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Write at least 250 words.
