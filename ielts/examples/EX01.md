# EX01 — Food: who eats what?

Original Vocora-commissioned fictional teaching material; not an IELTS test or a certified band exemplar.

Associated design lesson: **L0001**. Status: **authored reference pack, not a complete runtime lesson**.

## Target lexicon

| Target | Role | Meaning | Original example |

|---|---|---|---|

| bread | noun, normally uncountable in this meaning | a food made from flour and water and usually baked | I eat bread. |

| rice | uncountable noun | small grains cooked and eaten as food | We eat rice. |

| water | uncountable noun | the clear liquid people drink | I drink water. |

| milk | uncountable noun | a white liquid used as a drink or food | We drink milk. |

| eat | verb | to put food in your mouth and swallow it | I eat rice. |

| drink | verb | to take liquid into your mouth and swallow it | You drink water. |



**Word partners:** eat bread; eat rice; drink water


## Reading stimulus

My name is Mina. I eat bread in the morning. I drink milk. My brother, Sam, eats rice at lunch. He drinks water. We eat at home. Our table is small. In the evening, I eat rice and Sam eats bread. We both drink water. We like our meals. On Sunday, our friend Jo eats with us at home.


## Listening script — audio not yet recorded

Ari: Hello, Jo. What do you eat in the morning?
Jo: I eat rice.
Ari: Do you drink milk?
Jo: No. I drink water.
Ari: I eat bread and drink milk.
Jo: Do you eat at home?
Ari: Yes. My sister eats with me.
Jo: I eat with my brother. We sit at a small table.


## Reading questions and answer rationales

### EX01-R01 · `short-answer`

What does Mina eat in the morning? Write ONE WORD.

**Answer:** bread

**Why:** The second sentence states Mina eats bread in the morning.

### EX01-R02 · `short-answer`

What does Sam drink at lunch? Write ONE WORD.

**Answer:** water

**Why:** He in the sentence after Sam refers to Sam.

### EX01-R03 · `truth`

Mina and Sam eat at home. TRUE, FALSE or NOT GIVEN?

**Answer:** TRUE

**Why:** We eat at home explicitly states this.

### EX01-R04 · `truth`

Their table is large. TRUE, FALSE or NOT GIVEN?

**Answer:** FALSE

**Why:** The text says the table is small.

### EX01-R05 · `truth`

Jo is twenty years old. TRUE, FALSE or NOT GIVEN?

**Answer:** NOT GIVEN

**Why:** The text gives no age for Jo.

### EX01-R06 · `classification`

In I eat bread, identify the subject, verb and object.

**Answer:** I = subject; eat = verb; bread = object

**Why:** The subject does the action, and bread names what is eaten.

### EX01-R07 · `short-answer`

Who eats with the family on Sunday? Write ONE WORD.

**Answer:** Jo

**Why:** The last sentence identifies the visitor.

### EX01-R08 · `short-answer`

What do both Mina and Sam drink in the evening? Write ONE WORD.

**Answer:** water

**Why:** We both drink water follows the evening meal description.


## Listening questions and answer rationales

### EX01-A01 · `short-answer`

What does Jo eat in the morning? ONE WORD.

**Answer:** rice

**Why:** Jo says I eat rice.

### EX01-A02 · `short-answer`

What does Jo drink? ONE WORD.

**Answer:** water

**Why:** Jo rejects milk and states water.

### EX01-A03 · `short-answer`

What does Ari eat? ONE WORD.

**Answer:** bread

**Why:** Ari explicitly says I eat bread.

### EX01-A04 · `short-answer`

What does Ari drink? ONE WORD.

**Answer:** milk

**Why:** Ari says drink milk.

### EX01-A05 · `choice`

Who eats with Ari: his sister, his brother, or Jo?

**Answer:** his sister

**Why:** Ari says My sister eats with me.

### EX01-A06 · `choice`

Who eats with Jo: her teacher, her brother, or Mina?

**Answer:** her brother

**Why:** Jo names her brother.

### EX01-A07 · `truth`

Jo says she drinks milk. TRUE or FALSE?

**Answer:** FALSE

**Why:** No rejects the suggested milk; water is the final answer.

### EX01-A08 · `dictation`

Listen to the isolated phrase from a separate word-audio cue and type it: drink water.

**Answer:** drink water

**Why:** Exact word form and boundary, not a comprehension inference.


## Speaking

Say what you eat and drink in the morning. Use two or three complete statements. You may invent a meal. Do not read the model.


## Writing

Write 20–40 words about one meal. Say who eats, what they eat and what they drink. Use the six target words where relevant; do not force all of them.


### Original model — show after submission

I eat bread in the morning. I drink water. My sister eats rice. She drinks milk. We eat at home, and we like our food.


## Design and assessment note

Teach I/you/we, the core SVO frame, and eat versus drink before the reading. Names, home, morning and table must be known from onboarding or glossed. Third-person forms in the passage are supported exposure, not an untaught compulsory production target. TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN here is guided logic practice, not a timed IELTS test.
