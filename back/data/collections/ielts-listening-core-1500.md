## Money, banking and payments

- cash
  - definition: Money in the form of notes and coins.
  - example: I paid the taxi driver in cash.

- debit
  - definition: Money taken directly from a bank account when you pay for something.
  - example: The payment appeared as a debit on my account.

- credit card
  - definition: A card that lets you buy things now and pay the bank later.
  - example: I paid for the hotel with a credit card.

- cheque
  - definition: A written order telling a bank to pay a stated amount of money.
  - example: She sent the deposit by cheque.

- in advance
  - definition: Before an event, service, or deadline happens.
  - example: You need to pay the course fee in advance.

- annual fee
  - definition: A charge that must be paid once each year.
  - example: The account has an annual fee.

- monthly membership
  - definition: An arrangement in which you pay every month to belong to a club or service.
  - example: The gym offers a monthly membership.

- interest rate
  - definition: The percentage charged or paid when money is borrowed or saved.
  - example: The bank lowered its interest rate.

- deposit
  - definition: Money paid first to secure a booking, purchase, or rental.
  - example: We paid a deposit for the flat.

- tuition fees
  - definition: Money paid to a school, college, or university for teaching.
  - example: International students pay higher tuition fees.

- poverty
  - definition: The condition of having very little money and not enough for basic needs.
  - example: The charity works to reduce poverty.

- bank statement
  - definition: A document from a bank showing money paid into and out of an account.
  - example: I checked the charge on my bank statement.

- money management
  - definition: Planning and controlling how money is earned, saved, and spent.
  - example: The course teaches basic money management.

- current account
  - definition: A bank account used for everyday payments and withdrawals.
  - example: My salary is paid into my current account.

- student account
  - definition: A bank account designed for people who are studying.
  - example: The bank offers a free student account.

- withdraw
  - definition: To take money out of a bank account.
  - example: You can withdraw cash from the machine.

- low-risk investment
  - definition: A way of putting money somewhere with a small chance of losing it.
  - example: She chose a low-risk investment.

- mortgage
  - definition: A long-term loan used to buy a house or other property.
  - example: They took out a mortgage to buy their home.

- grace period
  - definition: Extra time after a payment is due when no penalty is charged.
  - example: The bank gives a ten-day grace period.

- budget deficit
  - definition: A situation in which planned spending is greater than expected income.
  - example: The government is trying to reduce the budget deficit.

- retail voucher
  - definition: A certificate or code from a shop that can be used instead of money.
  - example: I used a retail voucher to buy a jacket.

- coupon
  - definition: A small paper or code that gives a discount or special offer.
  - example: This coupon gives you ten percent off.

- counterfeit money
  - definition: Money made illegally to look like real money.
  - example: The shop refused the counterfeit money.

- public money
  - definition: Money that belongs to or is spent by the government for public purposes.
  - example: The project was paid for with public money.

- taxpayers' money
  - definition: Money collected through taxes and used by the government.
  - example: Officials must use taxpayers' money carefully.

- debt
  - definition: Money that is owed to another person, bank, or organisation.
  - example: He is trying to pay off his debt.

- interest-free credit
  - definition: A way to borrow money without paying extra interest for a set period.
  - example: The store offers interest-free credit for six months.

- purchase
  - definition: Something that you buy, or the act of buying it.
  - example: Keep the receipt for every purchase.

- partial refund
  - definition: Part of the money returned after you pay for something.
  - example: The airline gave us a partial refund.

- annuity
  - definition: A financial arrangement that pays a fixed amount regularly, often after retirement.
  - example: The pension includes an annuity.

- non-refundable
  - definition: Not able to be returned for your money back.
  - example: The cheapest ticket is non-refundable.

- distribution costs
  - definition: The money spent on moving goods from one place to another.
  - example: Fuel prices increased distribution costs.

- income
  - definition: Money that a person or business receives, especially from work or sales.
  - example: Her main income comes from teaching.

- finance department
  - definition: The part of an organisation that manages money, budgets, and payments.
  - example: Send the invoice to the finance department.

- family finances
  - definition: The way money is earned, spent, and managed in a household.
  - example: They discussed their family finances together.

- duty-free store
  - definition: A shop at an airport or border where some goods are sold without certain taxes.
  - example: She bought perfume at the duty-free store.

- price
  - definition: The amount of money asked for something that is sold.
  - example: The price includes breakfast.

- cost
  - definition: The amount of money needed to buy, make, or do something.
  - example: The total cost of the trip was £600.

- charge
  - definition: An amount of money that you must pay for a service or item.
  - example: There is an extra charge for luggage.

- payment
  - definition: Money given in exchange for goods, services, or money owed.
  - example: We received your payment yesterday.

- cash payment
  - definition: A payment made with notes or coins.
  - example: The driver only accepts cash payment.

- card payment
  - definition: A payment made using a bank card.
  - example: Card payment is available at the ticket desk.

- balance
  - definition: The amount of money currently available or owed in an account.
  - example: I checked my account balance online.

- account number
  - definition: A number used to identify a particular bank account.
  - example: Please enter your account number on the form.

- bank transfer
  - definition: Moving money electronically from one bank account to another.
  - example: I paid the rent by bank transfer.

- standing order
  - definition: An instruction to a bank to pay a fixed amount regularly.
  - example: I set up a standing order for the rent.

- direct debit
  - definition: An arrangement that lets a company take agreed payments from your bank account.
  - example: The electricity bill is paid by direct debit.

- overdraft
  - definition: An amount a bank allows you to spend when your account has no money left.
  - example: My bank gives me a small overdraft.

- savings account
  - definition: A bank account used mainly for keeping money and earning interest.
  - example: She puts part of her salary in a savings account.

- exchange rate
  - definition: The value of one country's money compared with another country's money.
  - example: The exchange rate changed before our trip.

- currency
  - definition: The system of money used in a particular country.
  - example: What currency do they use in Switzerland?

- pound
  - definition: The main unit of money in the United Kingdom.
  - example: The ticket costs one pound.

- dollar
  - definition: The main unit of money in the United States and several other countries.
  - example: The coffee costs one dollar.

- euro
  - definition: The main unit of money used in many European countries.
  - example: The museum ticket costs one euro.

- cent
  - definition: A small unit of money equal to one hundredth of a dollar, euro, or similar currency.
  - example: The price increased by one cent.

- penny
  - definition: A small British coin worth one hundredth of a pound.
  - example: I found a penny on the floor.

- receipt
  - definition: A paper or electronic record showing that you paid for something.
  - example: Keep your receipt in case you need a refund.

- invoice
  - definition: A document listing goods or services supplied and the amount to be paid.
  - example: The company emailed the invoice yesterday.

- bill
  - definition: A document showing how much money you owe for goods or services.
  - example: The gas bill was higher this month.

- refund
  - definition: Money returned to you after you return something or cancel a service.
  - example: The shop gave me a refund.

- full refund
  - definition: All of the money you paid returned to you.
  - example: We received a full refund after the flight was cancelled.

- discount
  - definition: A reduction in the usual price.
  - example: Students get a discount on tickets.

- concession
  - definition: A reduced price given to certain groups, such as students or older people.
  - example: There is a concession for people over sixty.

- voucher
  - definition: A paper or electronic code that can be exchanged for goods, services, or a discount.
  - example: I received a voucher for the restaurant.

- membership fee
  - definition: Money paid to belong to a club or organisation.
  - example: The annual membership fee is £40.

- entrance fee
  - definition: Money paid to enter a place or event.
  - example: The entrance fee includes a guided tour.

- admission fee
  - definition: Money paid for permission to enter a place or event.
  - example: Children pay a lower admission fee.

- booking fee
  - definition: An extra amount charged when you make a reservation.
  - example: There is a small booking fee for online tickets.

- cancellation fee
  - definition: Money charged when you cancel a booking or service.
  - example: We had to pay a cancellation fee.

- insurance premium
  - definition: The regular amount paid to an insurance company for cover.
  - example: My insurance premium went up this year.

- tax
  - definition: Money that people or businesses are required to pay to the government.
  - example: Income tax is taken from my salary.

- salary
  - definition: A fixed amount of money paid regularly for a job, usually monthly or yearly.
  - example: Her salary is paid at the end of each month.

- wage
  - definition: Money paid for work, often based on hours, days, or the amount of work done.
  - example: The company pays a higher wage for night work.

- installment / instalment
  - definition: One of several regular payments used to pay a total amount over time.
  - example: I paid the first instalment today.

- withdrawal
  - definition: The act of taking money out of a bank account.
  - example: There is a daily limit on cash withdrawal.

## School subjects and disciplines

- science
  - definition: The study of the natural and physical world through observation and experiments.
  - example: She plans to study science at university.

- politics
  - definition: The study or activity of government, power, and public decisions.
  - example: He is interested in politics.

- history
  - definition: The study of people and events in the past.
  - example: History was my favourite subject at school.

- biology
  - definition: The scientific study of living things.
  - example: She is studying biology.

- architecture
  - definition: The art and science of designing buildings.
  - example: He wants to study architecture.

- law
  - definition: The system of rules a country uses, and the study of those rules.
  - example: She studied law before becoming a lawyer.

- geography
  - definition: The study of places, people, and the physical features of the Earth.
  - example: We learned about rivers in geography.

- archaeology
  - definition: The study of past human life through objects, buildings, and remains.
  - example: He chose archaeology because he loves ancient history.

- literature
  - definition: Written works such as novels, poems, and plays, and the study of them.
  - example: She enjoys studying literature.

- business management
  - definition: The study of how organisations are planned, run, and developed.
  - example: He has a degree in business management.

- agriculture
  - definition: The work and science of growing crops and raising animals for food.
  - example: Modern agriculture uses new technology.

- statistics
  - definition: The study and use of numerical data to understand patterns and results.
  - example: Statistics is important in research.

- mathematics
  - definition: The study of numbers, quantities, shapes, and calculations.
  - example: Mathematics is required for this course.

- logic
  - definition: The study of clear reasoning and the rules used to reach conclusions.
  - example: The students learned basic logic.

- physics
  - definition: The scientific study of matter, energy, forces, and how they behave.
  - example: She finds physics difficult but interesting.

- psychology
  - definition: The scientific study of the mind and human behaviour.
  - example: He is taking a psychology course.

- anthropology
  - definition: The study of humans, cultures, and societies.
  - example: The lecture in anthropology was about family structures.

- economics
  - definition: The study of how money, goods, and services are produced and used.
  - example: She studies economics.

- philosophy
  - definition: The study of basic questions about knowledge, truth, values, and existence.
  - example: He enjoys reading philosophy.

- performing arts
  - definition: Creative activities performed for an audience, such as music, dance, and drama.
  - example: The college has a strong performing arts department.

- visual arts
  - definition: Creative art that is mainly seen, such as painting, drawing, and sculpture.
  - example: The course covers several forms of visual arts.

- chemistry
  - definition: The science of substances, their properties, and how they react with each other.
  - example: We did an experiment in chemistry.

- humanities
  - definition: Subjects that study human culture and ideas, such as history, languages, and literature.
  - example: She prefers the humanities to the sciences.
