# Vocora IELTS Academic

## L0001 — Food and everyday meals: Form and meaning

- bread
  - definition: A food made from flour and water and usually baked.
  - example: I eat bread in the morning.

- rice
  - definition: Small grains cooked and eaten as food.
  - example: We eat rice at lunch.

- water
  - definition: The clear liquid people drink.
  - example: They drink water with dinner.

- milk
  - definition: A white liquid used as a drink or food.
  - example: I drink milk at breakfast.

- eat
  - definition: To put food in your mouth and swallow it.
  - example: We eat at home.

- drink
  - definition: To take liquid into your mouth and swallow it.
  - example: You drink water in the morning.

- have breakfast
  - definition: To eat the first meal of the day.
  - example: I have breakfast before work.

- drink water
  - definition: To take water into your mouth and swallow it.
  - example: We drink water with our meals.
