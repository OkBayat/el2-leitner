# The future of food — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- food security
  - definition: Reliable access to enough safe and affordable food.
  - example: The council discussed how local farms could improve food security.

- worst-case scenario
  - definition: The most damaging possible outcome of a situation.
  - example: Our worst-case scenario was losing the entire harvest.

- stark
  - definition: Very clear and often unpleasant or serious.
  - example: The empty shelves were a stark reminder of the shortage.

- livestock
  - definition: Farm animals kept for food or other products.
  - example: The farmer moved the livestock to a sheltered field.

- piece of the puzzle
  - definition: One element needed to understand or solve a larger problem.
  - example: Better transport is only one piece of the puzzle.

- part of the solution, not the problem
  - definition: Contributing to an improvement instead of allowing a difficulty to continue.
  - example: By reducing waste, our team became part of the solution, not the problem.
