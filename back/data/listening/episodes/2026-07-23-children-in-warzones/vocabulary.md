# Children in warzones — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- hyper-vigilant
  - definition: Extremely alert to signs of possible danger.
  - example: After the break-in, Maya felt hyper-vigilant whenever she heard a noise outside.

- be of one mind
  - definition: To share the same opinion about a particular matter.
  - example: The whole committee seemed to be of one mind about protecting the park.

- confront
  - definition: To face and deal with something difficult instead of avoiding it.
  - example: We need to confront the problem before it becomes more serious.

- nightmare
  - definition: A frightening or deeply upsetting dream.
  - example: A nightmare woke me in the middle of the night.

- phenomenally
  - definition: To an unusually great or impressive degree.
  - example: The new library has been phenomenally popular with local families.

- resilient
  - definition: Able to recover or adapt after difficulties.
  - example: The small business proved resilient during a difficult year.
