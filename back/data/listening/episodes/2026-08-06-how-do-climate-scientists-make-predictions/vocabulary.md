# How do climate scientists make predictions? - Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- prediction
  - definition: A statement about an event that someone expects to happen later.
  - example: Her prediction of heavy rain proved correct.

- conditional
  - definition: Possible or valid only when another requirement is met.
  - example: Our trip is conditional on the weather improving.

- the thing about
  - definition: An informal way to point out an important feature of a person or subject.
  - example: The thing about this flat is that it gets plenty of sunlight.

- out of whack
  - definition: Not balanced, correctly arranged, or working as it should.
  - example: My sleep schedule is out of whack after the overnight flight.

- pro-science
  - definition: Supportive of scientific research and evidence; the prefix pro- expresses support.
  - example: The school promotes a pro-science approach to solving problems.

- not on the table
  - definition: Not available as a choice or not being considered.
  - example: A price increase is not on the table this year.
