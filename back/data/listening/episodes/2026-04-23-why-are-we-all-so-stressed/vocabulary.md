# Why are we all so stressed? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- chronic
  - definition: Continuing or returning over a long period.
  - example: The organisation faces a chronic shortage of staff.

- burnout
  - definition: Deep mental or physical exhaustion after prolonged pressure or overwork.
  - example: She recognised signs of burnout and discussed her workload with her manager.

- sharpen the mind
  - definition: Help someone think more clearly and concentrate.
  - example: A challenging puzzle can sharpen the mind.

- nerves
  - definition: Feelings of worry before an important or difficult event.
  - example: He took a slow breath to settle his nerves before speaking.

- one-size-fits-all
  - definition: Designed to apply the same approach to everyone or every situation.
  - example: A one-size-fits-all timetable does not suit every learner.

- to-do list
  - definition: A written record of tasks that still need to be completed.
  - example: I put the most urgent task at the top of my to-do list.
