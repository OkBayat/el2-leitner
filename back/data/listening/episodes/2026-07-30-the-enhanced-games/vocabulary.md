# The Enhanced Games — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- doping
  - definition: Using prohibited substances to improve performance in a sporting competition.
  - example: The athlete received a ban after an investigation into doping.

- it's fair to say
  - definition: A phrase introducing an opinion presented as reasonable or balanced.
  - example: After three successful concerts, it's fair to say the tour went well.

- divisive
  - definition: Causing strong disagreement between people.
  - example: The proposal to close the library became a divisive local issue.

- cross the line
  - definition: To go beyond what is considered acceptable behaviour.
  - example: Personal insults cross the line in a professional discussion.

- set a bad example
  - definition: To behave in a way that may encourage others to behave badly.
  - example: Drivers who ignore red lights set a bad example for younger road users.

- normalise
  - definition: To make something seem ordinary or acceptable when it was not seen that way before.
  - example: Regular conversations can help normalise asking for support.
