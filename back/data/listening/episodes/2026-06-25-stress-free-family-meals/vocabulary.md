# Stress-free family meals — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- ups and downs
  - definition: A changing mixture of better and worse experiences.
  - example: Our first year in business had plenty of ups and downs.

- mood
  - definition: The emotional state someone is in at a certain moment.
  - example: A walk in the sunshine improved my mood.

- battleground
  - definition: A place or situation in which people repeatedly argue.
  - example: The shared kitchen became a battleground over the washing-up.

- a big deal
  - definition: Something that matters a lot to someone.
  - example: Finishing her first race was a big deal to Maya.

- food allergy
  - definition: An immune reaction caused by eating a particular food.
  - example: He told the restaurant staff about his food allergy.

- winning formula
  - definition: An approach that repeatedly produces a good result.
  - example: Clear instructions and regular feedback proved a winning formula for the course.
