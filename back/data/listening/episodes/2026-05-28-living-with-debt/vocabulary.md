# Living with debt — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- debt
  - definition: Money that has been borrowed and must be returned.
  - example: They made a plan to reduce their debt gradually.

- pay off
  - definition: Return all the money owed on a loan or debt.
  - example: She hopes to pay off the loan next year.

- make ends meet
  - definition: Have enough money to cover essential everyday costs.
  - example: They took extra work to make ends meet.

- in arrears
  - definition: Late with payments that should already have been made.
  - example: The account was two months in arrears.

- the never-never
  - definition: An informal name for buying something through payments spread over time.
  - example: He regretted buying expensive furniture on the never-never.

- financial strain
  - definition: Pressure and worry caused by insufficient money.
  - example: The unexpected repair bill put the household under financial strain.
