# The power of poetry — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- open mic night
  - definition: An event at which members of the public can perform on stage.
  - example: She read her first poem at an open mic night.

- touch your soul
  - definition: Affect you very deeply emotionally.
  - example: A moving piece of music can touch your soul.

- jargon
  - definition: Specialist language used by a particular profession or group.
  - example: The trainer explained the technical jargon in everyday English.

- impenetrable
  - definition: So difficult or unclear that it cannot be understood easily.
  - example: Without an introduction, the report seemed impenetrable.

- turn-off
  - definition: Something that makes a person lose interest or enthusiasm.
  - example: The confusing instructions were a turn-off for new users.

- goosebumps / goose bumps
  - definition: Small raised bumps on the skin caused by cold, fear or strong emotion.
  - example: The final note of the song gave her goosebumps.
