# What's in a footballer's brain? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- going on
  - definition: Happening, especially when the situation is not fully understood.
  - example: The unusual noise made us wonder what was going on upstairs.

- expertise
  - definition: Deep knowledge or skill developed through learning and practice.
  - example: We relied on her expertise to repair the old clock.

- conscious
  - definition: Done with awareness and deliberate attention.
  - example: He made a conscious effort to speak more slowly.

- driven by
  - definition: Strongly influenced or motivated by something.
  - example: Her decision was driven by a desire to spend more time with family.

- all over the place
  - definition: Disorganised or unable to focus clearly.
  - example: After a sleepless night, my thoughts were all over the place.

- margin
  - definition: A difference between two results or levels that may determine an outcome.
  - example: Our team won the final by a very small margin.
