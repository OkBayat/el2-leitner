# How advertisers make us spend money — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- mental shortcut
  - definition: A way of deciding with less time or thought.
  - example: A familiar brand can become a mental shortcut when I am choosing tea.

- judged
  - definition: Feeling that other people are forming an opinion about you.
  - example: She felt judged when the assistant commented on her shopping.

- upsell
  - definition: Encourage a customer to buy an extra item or a costlier option.
  - example: The waiter tried to upsell a dessert after our main course.

- basket spend
  - definition: The total cost of the items bought in one purchase.
  - example: Adding batteries increased my basket spend by five pounds.

- think outside the box
  - definition: Look for an unusual or inventive way to solve a problem.
  - example: We need to think outside the box to fit a desk into this tiny room.

- model
  - definition: Demonstrate a way of behaving for others to follow.
  - example: Teachers can model patience when a student makes a mistake.
