# Limiting screen time for children — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- intentional
  - definition: Done deliberately, with a clear aim.
  - example: Taking a walk after lunch was an intentional choice.

- the bar needs to be higher
  - definition: The standard we accept should become stricter.
  - example: For apps designed for children, the bar needs to be higher.

- enable
  - definition: Give a person the means or chance to do a task.
  - example: These settings enable parents to limit screen time.

- eager
  - definition: Very keen to try or get something.
  - example: The children were eager to play outside.

- shift
  - definition: A change in a habit, position or way of thinking.
  - example: Leaving phones downstairs caused a small shift in our routine.

- set someone up for something
  - definition: Prepare a person for a future situation or task.
  - example: Good training can set someone up for something more demanding.
