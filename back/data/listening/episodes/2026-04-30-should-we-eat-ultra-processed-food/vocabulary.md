# Should we eat ultra-processed food? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- ready meal
  - definition: A prepared supermarket meal that only needs heating before it is eaten.
  - example: I bought a ready meal because I did not have time to cook.

- from scratch
  - definition: From the beginning, without relying on something already prepared.
  - example: She made the soup from scratch using fresh vegetables.

- guilty pleasure
  - definition: Something enjoyable that a person feels slightly embarrassed or guilty about liking.
  - example: Watching old game shows is his guilty pleasure after work.

- once in a while
  - definition: Occasionally rather than regularly.
  - example: We order takeaway food once in a while.

- from time to time
  - definition: Sometimes, but not very often.
  - example: I work from a café from time to time.

- everything in moderation
  - definition: Advice that avoiding excessive amounts is usually more sensible than having too much of anything.
  - example: She enjoys dessert but follows the idea of everything in moderation.
