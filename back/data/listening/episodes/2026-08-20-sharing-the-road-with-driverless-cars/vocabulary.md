# Sharing the road with driverless cars — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- eye contact
  - definition: Looking directly into another person's eyes.
  - example: The speaker made eye contact with people around the room.

- wave
  - definition: To move a raised hand to greet someone or communicate a message.
  - example: Please wave when you see our car arrive.

- alert
  - definition: To warn someone about a possible danger or important problem.
  - example: The alarm will alert staff if smoke is detected.

- animation
  - definition: A sequence of images displayed to create the appearance of movement.
  - example: The animation shows how water moves through the machine.

- glance
  - definition: A very quick look at something.
  - example: I took a glance at the timetable before boarding the train.

- jaywalking
  - definition: Crossing a road where it is prohibited or in an unsafe way.
  - example: The visitor asked which local rules applied to jaywalking.
