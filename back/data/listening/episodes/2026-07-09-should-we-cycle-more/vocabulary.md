# Should we cycle more? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- deeply ingrained
  - definition: So firmly established that it is difficult to change.
  - example: Arriving early is a deeply ingrained habit in my family.

- take off
  - definition: Begin to grow rapidly in popularity or success.
  - example: Her online bakery began to take off after a local review.

- milestone
  - definition: An event that marks an important stage of progress.
  - example: Moving into our own office was a milestone for the company.

- quality of life
  - definition: How comfortable, healthy and enjoyable someone's life is.
  - example: A shorter commute improved his quality of life.

- liveability
  - definition: How comfortable and practical a place is to live in.
  - example: Clean parks and reliable buses improve a city's liveability.

- a copy and paste
  - definition: An exact reproduction placed into a different context.
  - example: The second proposal was a copy and paste of the first.
