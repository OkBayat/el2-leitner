# Love the foods you hate — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- hard-wired
  - definition: Built into the way someone or something naturally responds.
  - example: His hard-wired preference for routine made sudden changes difficult.

- via
  - definition: By means of something or by passing through a place.
  - example: Please send the application via email.

- masking
  - definition: Covering or hiding an unwanted quality.
  - example: The strong sauce was masking the flavour of the vegetables.

- inherently
  - definition: As a natural or essential part of something.
  - example: A new idea is not inherently better than an old one.

- dodgy
  - definition: Unreliable or of doubtful quality; used of food that may be unsafe.
  - example: The milk smelled dodgy, so I did not use it.

- avoidance
  - definition: The act of deliberately staying away from something.
  - example: His avoidance of difficult conversations delayed the decision.
