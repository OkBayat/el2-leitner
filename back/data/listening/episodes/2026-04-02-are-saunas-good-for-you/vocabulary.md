  - example: The expensive hotel is not all it's cracked up to be.

- at ease
  - definition: Feeling calm and comfortable in a situation.
  - example: The friendly welcome immediately put the new student at ease.

- tense
  - definition: Worried or physically tight rather than relaxed.
  - example: She felt tense before the interview.

- reset
  - definition: Recover and make a fresh start after a pause.
  - example: A quiet weekend helped me reset before the new project.

- observational data
  - definition: Information recorded while watching what happens without controlling the situation.
  - example: The researchers collected observational data during the school day.

- turn your nose up at
  - definition: Reject something because you consider it inferior.
  - example: Do not turn your nose up at the soup before you taste it.
