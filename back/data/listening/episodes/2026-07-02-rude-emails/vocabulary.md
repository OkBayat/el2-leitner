# Rude emails — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- put someone on the defensive
  - definition: Make a person feel they must protect or justify themselves.
  - example: An unfair accusation can put someone on the defensive.

- uncivil
  - definition: Rude or lacking basic courtesy.
  - example: The manager apologised for his uncivil reply.

- belittle
  - definition: Treat a person or their achievements as having little value.
  - example: A good coach does not belittle beginners when they make mistakes.

- put someone in their place
  - definition: Make a person aware that you consider their status or importance lower than they think.
  - example: She disliked meetings where people tried to put someone in their place.

- get something off your plate
  - definition: Remove a task from your own responsibilities, often by passing it on.
  - example: Delegating can help you get something off your plate during a busy week.

- frazzled
  - definition: Worn out and unsettled by too many demands.
  - example: After three urgent deadlines, I felt completely frazzled.
