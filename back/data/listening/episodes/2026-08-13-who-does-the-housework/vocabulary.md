# Who does the housework? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- chores
  - definition: Routine jobs that need to be done around the home.
  - example: We finish our chores on Saturday morning before going out.

- fair share
  - definition: A reasonable portion of the work, cost or responsibility.
  - example: Everyone on the team should do a fair share of the preparation.

- women's work
  - definition: A stereotyped label for tasks assumed to be the responsibility of women.
  - example: Calling cleaning women's work ignores everyone's responsibility for a shared home.

- division of labour
  - definition: An arrangement in which different people or groups perform different tasks.
  - example: A clear division of labour helped the volunteers organise the event.

- take a breather
  - definition: To pause an activity briefly in order to rest.
  - example: We stopped halfway up the hill to take a breather.

- pull your weight
  - definition: To contribute your reasonable share of the work in a group.
  - example: When you share a flat, you need to pull your weight with the cleaning.
