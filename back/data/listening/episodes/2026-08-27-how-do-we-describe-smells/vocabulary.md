# How do we describe smells? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- stinky
  - definition: Having a strong, unpleasant smell; an informal word.
  - example: We left the stinky trainers outside to air overnight.

- fragrant
  - definition: Having a pleasant, often sweet smell.
  - example: The fragrant herbs filled the kitchen with a fresh scent.

- subjective
  - definition: Based on personal feelings or opinions rather than an independent measurement.
  - example: Choosing the most beautiful painting is a subjective judgement.

- visual
  - definition: Connected with seeing or with images.
  - example: The teacher used visual clues to explain the new words.

- auditory
  - definition: Connected with hearing or sound.
  - example: The museum offers both visual and auditory experiences.

- shut something down
  - definition: To stop an activity, discussion or process.
  - example: A manager should not shut something down simply because an idea is unfamiliar.
