# Searching for life on another planet — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- habitable
  - definition: Suitable for living in.
  - example: The damaged house will not be habitable until the roof is repaired.

- zone
  - definition: An area defined by a particular feature or purpose.
  - example: Cars are not allowed in the pedestrian zone.

- potential
  - definition: Possible in the future but not yet certain or achieved.
  - example: The team discussed a potential solution to the problem.

- rule out
  - definition: Decide that an explanation or possibility can be rejected.
  - example: We cannot rule out a delay until the train arrives.

- dominated
  - definition: Had the greatest influence or occupied the largest part.
  - example: One subject dominated the conversation throughout dinner.

- fingers crossed
  - definition: An expression of hope that something will happen successfully.
  - example: Fingers crossed that the weather stays dry for the picnic.
