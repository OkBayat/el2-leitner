# Making cities feel quieter — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- decibel
  - definition: A unit used to express the level of a sound.
  - example: The meter displayed the decibel level beside the road.

- dress loud
  - definition: Wear clothes with striking colours or bold patterns.
  - example: The performers like to dress loud when they appear on stage.

- handle
  - definition: Manage or deal with a difficult situation.
  - example: She learned to handle complaints calmly.

- art installation
  - definition: A work of art arranged for people to experience in a particular space.
  - example: The art installation filled the hall with moving lights.

- squeaky
  - definition: Making a thin, high-pitched sound.
  - example: The squeaky door woke everyone in the house.

- counterintuitive
  - definition: Contrary to what someone would first expect.
  - example: The counterintuitive result made the researchers check their calculations again.
