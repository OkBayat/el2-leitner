# How is climate change affecting extreme weather? — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- likelihood
  - definition: The chance that an event will happen.
  - example: Warm seas can increase the likelihood of powerful storms.

- landslide
  - definition: A large amount of earth or rock falling down a slope.
  - example: Heavy rain caused a landslide near the village.

- mudslide
  - definition: Wet soil flowing quickly down a hill.
  - example: The road closed after a mudslide covered both lanes.

- storm surge
  - definition: A rise in coastal water driven by a storm.
  - example: The storm surge flooded several homes near the beach.

- sweep in
  - definition: Arrive or move into an area with speed and force.
  - example: Strong winds can sweep in from the sea without warning.

- casualty
  - definition: A person hurt or killed in a dangerous event.
  - example: The injured driver was the only casualty of the accident.
