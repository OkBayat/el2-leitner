# How reading shapes your brain — Vocabulary

## Episode vocabulary

- natural
  - definition: Present through nature rather than created by special training or intervention.
  - example: The room was bright with natural light.

- dedicated
  - definition: Set aside or designed for a particular purpose.
  - example: The library has a dedicated space for quiet study.

- co-opt
  - definition: Bring someone or something into use for a new purpose.
  - example: The organisers decided to co-opt two volunteers into the planning team.

- circuit
  - definition: A connected system through which something can move or communicate.
  - example: The technician checked every connection in the circuit.

- inevitably
  - definition: In a way that cannot realistically be prevented.
  - example: Leaving the work until the final evening inevitably caused problems.

- intricate
  - definition: Containing many small and carefully connected details.
  - example: She admired the intricate pattern on the carpet.
